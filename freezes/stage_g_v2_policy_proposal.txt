Stage G v2 Tiered Support Policy Proposal
=========================================

Scope
-----
Stage G v2 is a support and prioritization review layer only. It does not retrain, fine-tune, or replace the frozen flux model, and it must not update final_candidate_master_ledger.csv.

Manual-label guardrail
----------------------
Stage G v2 must not override manual labels, Stage D/F vetting, visual review, or final candidate ledger decisions. Scores are used only to route review attention.

Tier definitions
----------------
- strong_support: calibrated_score >= 0.70
- review_support: 0.45 <= calibrated_score < 0.70
- weak_support: calibrated_score < 0.45

Required actions
----------------
- candidate_like / planet_like rows below 0.70: candidate_retained_stage_g_review_needed
- uncertain_hold / not_science_like rows above 0.70: high_score_false_positive_or_recheck_required
- EPIC_211842439: high-score false positive / do-not-promote
- EPIC_211954033, EPIC_211889692, EPIC_211908778, EPIC_211404127, and EPIC_211759361: feature review for Stage G suppression
