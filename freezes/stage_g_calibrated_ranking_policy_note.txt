Stage G Calibrated Ranking Policy Note
======================================

Inputs
------
This policy note summarizes the current Stage G calibrated ranking pass using:

- freezes/stage_g_calibrated_ranking_layer.csv
- freezes/stage_g_calibrated_ranking_layer_rule.json
- freezes/stage_g_candidate_ledger_review.csv
- the Stage G feature-attribution breakdown for the below-threshold
  science_like rows and the false-positive pass row

Model Status
------------
Stage G is a tabular ranking and support layer only. It is not a replacement
for the Keras flux model, manual labels, Stage D/F vetting, or final candidate
ledger decisions.

The active K2 Keras flux model remains frozen:

models/k2_nocrop_flux_seed46_split303.best.keras

Do not retrain or fine-tune any model as part of this Stage G policy.

Policy Conclusions
------------------
Stage G score must not override manual labels.

Candidate-like and planet-like rows are not demoted solely because
calibrated_score < 0.70. A below-threshold Stage G score should be treated as
a request for explanation, feature review, or follow-up prioritization, not as
an automatic rejection.

Uncertain_hold and not_science_like rows are not promoted solely because
calibrated_score >= 0.70. A high Stage G score can flag a row for review, but
it cannot overturn closed labels or manual/visual false-positive decisions by
itself.

EPIC_211842439 is a known false-positive pass under the current Stage G rule.
It scored above the calibrated threshold because Stage D and Stage F support
were strong and penalties were small, but its closed label is not_science_like
and its ledger status is hold_low_priority_candidate. It must remain
do-not-promote unless new external evidence or manual review explicitly
changes that status.

Over-Penalized Science-Like Rows
--------------------------------
The current Stage G rule over-penalizes several science_like rows, especially
through odd/even penalties and Stage F weakness terms:

- EPIC_211908778
- EPIC_211404127
- EPIC_211759361
- EPIC_211889692
- EPIC_211954033

These rows should not be treated as rejected or demoted solely because their
calibrated_score is below 0.70. Their feature breakdowns show that the current
rule is conservative and can suppress known or candidate-like positives when
odd/even, OOT, Stage F, or Stage D components are imperfect.

Operational Use
---------------
Use Stage G as a prioritization and support score, not as a hard
acceptance/rejection gate.

Recommended interpretation:

- calibrated_score >= 0.70: high-priority review/support signal, not automatic
  promotion.
- calibrated_score < 0.70 for science_like rows: inspect suppression reasons;
  do not demote solely from the Stage G score.
- flux_p_science_like >= 0.60: broad send-to-vetting signal, consistent with
  the frozen Keras flux-model policy.
- manual labels, Stage D/F diagnostics, visual review, and external evidence
  remain authoritative for promotion/rejection decisions.

Ledger Policy
-------------
Do not update final_candidate_master_ledger.csv from this Stage G score alone.
Any ledger change must be a separate deliberate review action with supporting
evidence beyond the calibrated ranking score.
