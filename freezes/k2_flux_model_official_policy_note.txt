Official K2 Flux-Model Policy Note
===================================

Active Model
------------
The active existing K2 flux model is:

models/k2_nocrop_flux_seed46_split303.best.keras

This model is frozen for the current Stage G work. Do not train or fine-tune a
new Keras model from the Stage F closed-45 freeze at this time.

Score Semantics
---------------
The model produces flux_p_science_like. Fresh scores from the active model
match the existing stored flux_p_science_like values exactly, so the stored
score is the canonical flux-model score for downstream ranking and triage.

Operational Policy
------------------
Use flux_p_science_like as a broad recall / triage signal, not as an
auto-promotion classifier.

- Around 0.60 is the useful "send to deeper vetting" threshold.
- Scores at 0.70 and above are interesting, but still require Stage D/F checks.
- Do not auto-promote from flux score alone.
- Promotion and rejection decisions must use Stage D/F metrics, false-positive
  diagnostics, visual/manual review, and external vetting evidence where
  available.
